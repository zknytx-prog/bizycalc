How this calculator works

Stripe's published US standard pricing is 2.9% + $0.30 for a successful domestic card transaction. International cards add 1.5%. If currency conversion is required, Stripe lists an additional 1% fee. The calculator applies only the options you select.

Worked example

A $500 domestic card payment has an estimated fee of $14.80, leaving $485.20. If the card is international, the 1.5% international-card charge adds $7.50. If currency conversion is also required, another 1% of the transaction amount is added.

Source and assumptions

Last reviewed: September 16, 2026. This tool uses Stripe's US standard card pricing. Custom contracts, other payment methods, manually entered cards, disputes, subscriptions, Connect, or other Stripe products can have different fees.

FAQ

Does Stripe charge a monthly fee for standard card processing? Stripe's standard pay-as-you-go card pricing does not require a standard monthly processing fee, although other Stripe products can have separate charges.

What about ACH? ACH and other bank-based payment methods use different pricing and are not calculated here.

Can my actual rate be lower? Yes. Stripe offers custom pricing for businesses with qualifying payment volume or specialized needs.
