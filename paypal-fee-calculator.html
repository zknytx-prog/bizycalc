How this calculator works

The calculator applies the published US percentage and fixed fee for the payment type you select. If the payer and receiver are in different markets, the international commercial transaction surcharge is added. Currency conversion is not added because conversion pricing depends on the currencies and PayPal's applicable exchange-rate terms.

Worked example

For a $500 domestic PayPal Checkout payment, the estimated fee is 3.49% of $500 ($17.45) plus $0.49, or $17.94. The estimated net is $482.06. If the same payment is an international commercial transaction, another 1.50% of the payment amount is added.

Source and assumptions

Last reviewed: September 16, 2026. Rates are based on PayPal's published US merchant fee table. PayPal offers multiple products and special rates, so confirm the rate that applies to your account before pricing a material transaction.

FAQ

Does this include currency conversion? No. Currency conversion can add cost, but the amount depends on the currencies and PayPal terms in effect for the transaction.

Does it cover every PayPal product? No. PayPal Pay Later, QR, invoicing, charity, advanced card products, and negotiated merchant rates can differ.

Why can my actual fee differ? Account type, product, country, currency, transaction status, and negotiated pricing can all change the final fee.
