Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use BizyCalc.com.

Calculator inputs

BizyCalc calculators run in your browser using client-side JavaScript. The numbers you type into calculator fields are used to generate the result on your device. BizyCalc does not operate an application server that receives or stores those calculator inputs.

Analytics and site measurement

BizyCalc uses Google Tag Manager to manage site tags and uses Ahrefs Web Analytics to understand aggregate traffic and page usage. These services may process technical information such as page views, device/browser information, referring pages, approximate location derived from network information, and similar usage data according to their own terms and privacy practices.

Advertising technology

BizyCalc includes Google AdSense code as part of its advertising and monetization setup. If and when Google serves ads on the site, Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize advertising where permitted. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services and manage ad personalization at Google Ads Settings.

Cookies and browser controls

Third-party analytics or advertising services may set or read cookies as allowed by your browser settings and applicable consent requirements. You can control or delete cookies through your browser and through the privacy controls offered by the relevant service providers.

Third-party links

Some calculator pages link to official pricing pages, government information, marketplace documentation, or other external resources so users can verify assumptions. BizyCalc is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external websites.

Children's privacy

BizyCalc is intended for general business, freelance, and seller use and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information through the site.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site's analytics, advertising, or other services change. The date at the top of this page shows the latest revision.
