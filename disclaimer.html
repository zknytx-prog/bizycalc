Disclaimer

BizyCalc calculators are designed for quick planning and comparison. They are not a replacement for professional advice or for the final figures provided by a marketplace, payment processor, lender, carrier, customs authority, or tax agency.

Estimates, not guarantees

Results are calculated from the values you enter and the assumptions displayed on the relevant page. Small differences in rates, rounding, minimum fees, category rules, account status, geographic location, discounts, taxes, or provider policies can change the real amount.

Marketplace and payment fees

Amazon, Etsy, eBay, PayPal, Stripe, Shopify, Wise, and other providers may change fees or apply different pricing by country, product category, plan, payment method, seller status, or transaction type. Where a BizyCalc page uses published provider rates, use the linked official source to confirm the current rule before pricing a meaningful transaction.

Tax, VAT, GST, and import calculations

Generic tax, VAT, GST, sales-tax, and import-duty tools only calculate the rate or assumptions you provide. They do not determine whether a transaction is taxable, which jurisdiction applies, whether exemptions are available, or what customs classification should be used.

Loans and financial planning

Loan and payback calculations are mathematical estimates. Actual lender disclosures can include fees, compounding conventions, payment timing, late charges, or other terms not represented in a simplified planning tool.

Professional advice

Nothing on BizyCalc is financial, legal, tax, accounting, investment, or other professional advice. For decisions with material financial or legal consequences, verify the figures with the relevant provider or a qualified professional.
