How this calculator works

Shopify's US pricing page lists standard online card rates that decrease by plan: 2.9% + $0.30 on Basic, 2.7% + $0.30 on Grow, and 2.5% + $0.30 on Advanced. Shopify also lists an additional 1% for international online card rates. This tool applies those published assumptions.

Worked example

On an $80 domestic online sale, Basic produces an estimated fee of $2.62 and an estimated net of $77.38. On Advanced, the same domestic sale produces an estimated fee of $2.30. Plan subscription costs are not included in either result.

Third-party payment providers

If Shopify Payments is not used, Shopify may charge an additional third-party transaction fee. Shopify currently lists 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow, and 0.6% on Advanced. Those charges are separate from this Shopify Payments calculator and from the third-party processor's own fees.

Source and assumptions

Last reviewed: September 16, 2026. Rates shown are US standard online card rates published by Shopify. Premium cards, other countries, POS transactions, Plus/custom pricing, currency conversion, subscriptions, and other payment methods can differ.

FAQ

Does this include the monthly Shopify subscription? No. This page isolates transaction processing fees.

Do POS rates differ? Yes. Card-present pricing is different from online card pricing.

Can Shopify rates change? Yes. Verify the current pricing page before making a material pricing or plan decision.
